About us

We’re from here. That’s the difference.

Harmony Excursions is a Dominican team based in Punta Cana. We started because we wanted visitors to see the country we know — not just the hotel beach.

01

Local, not generic

Our guides are Dominican. They know which beach is quiet on a Tuesday, where the best fried fish is and how to tell a good cigar from a souvenir one.

02

Honest prices

Every price on this site is in US dollars and shows what is included. If something costs extra, we say it before you book.

03

Small details, big day

Pickup at your hotel, cold drinks on the boat, time to actually swim. We plan the boring parts so you can enjoy the rest.

04

Respect for the places we visit

Saona, Catalina and Los Haitises are protected parks. We follow park rules and ask our guests to use reef-safe sunscreen.

What we care about

  • Safety first
  • Real hospitality
  • Fair prices
  • Protecting our coast
