Adventure

Buggy Adventure

Punta Cana Half day Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the tour.

Drive your own buggy through mud trails, rivers and countryside villages.

Overview

Get behind the wheel and follow your guide off-road through jungle trails, sugar-cane fields and river crossings. Expect to come back covered in mud and smiling.

Stops include a typical Dominican house to taste coffee and cacao, and a cave or beach to cool off.

Highlights

  • Drive your own off-road buggy
  • River crossings and mud trails
  • Coffee and cacao tasting
  • Cave or beach stop

Included

  • Round-trip hotel transport
  • Expert guide
  • Buggy and helmet
  • Tasting at a local house

Not included

  • Bandana and goggles
  • Photos and videos
  • Tips
