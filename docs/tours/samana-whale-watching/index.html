Samaná Whale Watching

Samaná Full day Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the tour.

Every winter thousands of humpback whales come to Samaná Bay to mate and give birth.

Overview

Samaná Bay is one of the best places on earth to see humpback whales. From the boat you can watch them breach, slap their tails and swim with their calves.

This tour only runs during whale season. Message us to check dates before you book.

Season: Humpback whale season: mid-January to late March

Highlights

  • Humpback whales in the wild
  • Mothers with their calves
  • Responsible whale-watching boat
  • Samaná Bay scenery
