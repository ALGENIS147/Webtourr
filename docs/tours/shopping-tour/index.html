Local Shopping Tour

Punta Cana Half day Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the tour.

Free with any excursion: cigars, larimar, cacao and handmade souvenirs.

Overview

Your guide takes you to local markets and artisan shops to find authentic Dominican products — cigars, larimar jewellery, coffee, cacao, mamajuana and handmade crafts — and tells you the stories behind them.
