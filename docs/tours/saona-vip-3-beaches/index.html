Saona Island VIP: 3 Beaches

Isla Saona Full day · 6:00 a.m. – 7:00 p.m. Cancel before 5:00 p.m. the day before for a 75% refund (25% is non-refundable). Monday to Saturday

Three beaches, a fishing village, natural pools with starfish and a seafront buffet — all inclusive.

Overview

Saona was chosen as a filming location for Pirates of the Caribbean — and once you see it, you’ll understand why. This VIP version visits three beaches instead of one, spending about 1 hour and 30 minutes at each, with plenty of time to swim, take photos and relax with no rush.

You’ll stop at Flamingo Beach, have a buffet lunch in the fishing village of Mano Juan, look for sea turtles at the protected sanctuary, cross the Catuano Channel and the mangroves, and swim next to starfish in the natural pools. If there is sargassum or bad weather, that stop moves to Playa Abanico so you always get the best possible day.

Highlights

  • Three different beaches, about 1 h 30 min at each
  • Buffet lunch facing the sea in Mano Juan
  • Natural pools with starfish
  • Open bar with Brugal and Barceló rum

What you’ll visit

  1. 🏝Flamingo BeachOne of the most beautiful beaches on Saona.
  2. 🏡Mano Juan fishing villageWhere the buffet lunch is served.
  3. 🐢Sea turtle sanctuaryYou may see turtles if they are in the protected area that day. It is a sanctuary, not an aquarium, so sightings are not guaranteed.
  4. 🏝Playa del Canto
  5. 🌊Catuano ChannelPanoramic views.
  6. 🎋Protected mangroves
  7. ⭐Natural poolsSwim next to the starfish and watch them — touching them is not allowed.
