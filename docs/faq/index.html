Help

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Write to us on WhatsApp.

Do you pick us up at the hotel?

Yes. Most experiences include round-trip transport from hotels in Punta Cana, Bávaro, Cap Cana & Uvero Alto. After you book we send you the exact pickup time. If your hotel is elsewhere, write to us and we will tell you if we can reach it.

How do I pay?

You do not pay anything online. You send a booking request, we confirm availability, and we agree the payment method with you on WhatsApp or by email.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel for free up to 24 hours before the tour. If we cancel because of weather or safety, you can choose another date or get a full refund.

Are prices per person?

Shared tours are priced per person, with a lower price for children. Private boats and private tours are priced per group. Every booking form shows the total before you send it.

Can children join?

Yes, on most tours. Children aged 3–11 pay the child price. For diving and some adventure activities there are minimum ages — ask us.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, a towel, reef-safe sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and some cash for tips, photos and souvenirs.

What if it rains?

Tropical showers are usually short and tours run as normal. If the sea or weather is unsafe, the captain or guide decides, and we will reschedule or refund you.

Do you organise private groups and events?

Yes — private speedboats for 6 to 20 guests, party boats for up to 45, and private city tours. Tell us your group size and date.

Your next adventure is one message away.

Tell us your dates and how many you are. We’ll suggest the best day for you.
