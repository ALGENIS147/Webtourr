Only in the Dominican Republic

The flavour you take home

Every tour comes with a little piece of Dominican life. These are the things our guests remember long after the tan fades.

  • Music

    Merengue & bachata

    Both born here and both UNESCO cultural heritage. You will hear them on every boat, in every colmado — and someone will teach you the steps.

    Saona Island Classic

  • Gemstone

    Larimar

    A sea-blue stone found in only one place on Earth: the mountains of Barahona, in the south of the country.

    Local Shopping Tour

  • Lunch

    La Bandera

    Rice, red beans and stewed meat — the national lunch is named after the flag. You will taste it on our tours.

    Santo Domingo Colonial City

  • Harvest

    Cacao & café

    The Dominican Republic is one of the world’s biggest growers of organic cacao. Taste it straight from the farm.

    Hacienda Park 7-in-1 Adventure

  • Drink

    Mamajuana

    Rum, red wine and honey soaked with tree bark and herbs. Every family swears its recipe is the best.

    Local Shopping Tour

  • On the beach

    Coco frío

    A green coconut, cut open with a machete in front of you. The most Dominican drink there is.

    Saona Island Classic

Good to know

Questions travellers ask us

All questions
Do you pick us up at the hotel?

Yes. Most experiences include round-trip transport from hotels in Punta Cana, Bávaro, Cap Cana & Uvero Alto. After you book we send you the exact pickup time. If your hotel is elsewhere, write to us and we will tell you if we can reach it.

How do I pay?

You do not pay anything online. You send a booking request, we confirm availability, and we agree the payment method with you on WhatsApp or by email.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel for free up to 24 hours before the tour. If we cancel because of weather or safety, you can choose another date or get a full refund.

Are prices per person?

Shared tours are priced per person, with a lower price for children. Private boats and private tours are priced per group. Every booking form shows the total before you send it.
